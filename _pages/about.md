Junteng Liu is a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where he is advised by Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He also previously advised him during his undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).

He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where he received his B.Eng. (2020-2024).

**Research interests**

His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

**Academic background**

* Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024 - Present
* B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020 - 2024 (graduated June 2024)

**Research experience**

* Research Intern, MINIMAX, February 2025 - Present

* Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab, June 2023 - December 2023 (Advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng)
