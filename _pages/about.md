# Junteng Liu

First-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/HKUST-NLP/nlp-group), led by Professor Junxian He. Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Professor Junxian He in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/HKUST-NLP/nlp-group). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

Previously, I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.
